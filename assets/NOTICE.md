Reused physical/collision definition per wheel:
- radius 0.033 m; cylinder length 0.018 m
- mass 0.028498940 kg
- rolling-axis inertia Izz = 2.0712558e-5 kg m^2
- continuous joint about its local z axis

wheel_source_excerpt.urdf is an excerpt, NOT the complete original URDF file.
wheel.obj, wheel.mtl and src/wheel_data.js are LOCAL dimensional reconstructions:
tire/rim/hub/tread surfaces generated by make_wheel.py for a lightweight Canvas
rendering. Radius and width follow the sourced physical model. Hub/screw/tread
features are illustrative and not claimed as original CAD geometry.

The original 1,083,684-byte left_tire.stl could NOT be downloaded in this runtime;
it is NOT bundled. It was never claimed to have been fetched or rendered.
No tire contact/slip mesh, mounting compatibility, mass extraction from OBJ,
manufacturing drawing, or real wheel/servo assembly validation is provided.
The actual rotating image uses x / radius. Mass and inertia come from the URDF,
not the approximate visual mesh. User cart mass includes wheel translating mass;
rotating wheel and rotor inertia are added separately, not double counted.
